## Different from yours, and why

**Underneath (a photographer never sees these)**
- **The vault is a real post kit folder, "My vault", in Your Content.** Its photo list points at each shoot's own photos; no photo is copied to be in it. That is why the reel page, the designers, the words page and Add to my queue all work in it with nothing new.
- **Photo numbers in the vault never move,** as in every kit since v27. A photo that goes and comes back has its old number.
- **A clip from a shoot stays in that shoot.** The vault lists it and keeps its own small pictures of it.
- **Looking at photos** uses the Mac's own image classifier on the small copies only. Nothing leaves the computer, and no original is opened.

**Things a photographer could notice**
- **Archived post kits are in the vault too.** Your mock-up left them out. Ours keeps them, so archiving a kit can never change a reel that used its photos.
- **A post kit joins the vault once its own page has been opened at least once** (it needs its numbered photo list, and the vault never writes into another kit to make one).
- **Remove from my vault can be undone.** Put it back sits right beside the line, and a small fold at the bottom, "Taken out of my vault (1)", lists what was removed. Yours had no way back.
- **In a search, a shoot with nothing found stays in the list,** shut and greyed. Your mock-up hides it; your notes say "leaves the rest shut". We followed the notes.
- **+ Add a shoot copies that folder's finished photos into the vault** (checked, originals untouched) and shows them under Added to my vault. It does not make a new post kit. A shoot that already has a post kit is refused: "That shoot is already in your vault, from its post kit."
- **+ Add clips opens the vault's Footage folder.** The Assistant copies no video.
- **Talking to camera in the vault opens the talking page we already have.** Your "Your talking video" heading and paragraph are kept but shown nowhere yet.
- **Both designers show every vault photo in one tray.** Your folds and search are on the reel page only. The first carousel design in the vault opens blank.
- **One of a thing is singular:** "1 photo · 1 clip", "1 found". Yours says "1 photos".
- **"clips" in the strip counts videos,** in the vault's Footage folder and every post kit's. Yours counted clips already cut.
- **Use it in a reel** shows for a clip only, and opens the reel chooser without carrying the clip along.
- **No serif:** your italic accent words are in the plain font, in coral, as on v28.
- **Your line on the Back up a card screen,** "Your assistant asks a quick question or two, then you can walk away...", is no longer true (see below), so a line of ours stands there: "Your assistant finds your card and starts copying. There's nothing to answer first, so you can walk away. The report is ready when you're back, and it shows up below."
- **"Step 1 of your workflow"** is gone from the Back up a card screen, since the home page no longer numbers steps. The post kits screen takes your title, "Turn your shoots into posts."

## The backup, changed today (Dustin's decision, from your "overwhelmed")

You said the run of questions before your first backup was too much. Dustin: zero questions before the copy, options on the report afterwards.

- **"backup now" just starts.** It finds the card, checks quietly, says what it found and that you can walk away.
- **A first backup may ask one thing, once ever: which drive.** With one external drive plugged in it asks nothing. With several it asks which. With none it asks where the photos should go.
- **Chosen for you the first time:** right on the drive (or inside a folder plainly named for shoots, like Photo Shoots), folders named year first, photos named for the shoot, no second copy.
- **On the report afterwards, "Anything you'd like to change?":** Make a second copy (with "Do this after every backup"), This shoot continues on another card, Change how my shoots are named, Use a different drive next time, and Write a note to my client on each shoot.
- **Gone:** the setup conversation, "Same as usual?", "How many memory cards", "What's on the card?", the client-voice question (asked the first time you ask for a note), and your Nov 13 "here's what's about to happen" paragraph.
- **Every backup ends with two buttons:** Show the report, and Eject my card.

Every word in the report's new options is ours and a draft, about 90 lines. They are yours to reword.

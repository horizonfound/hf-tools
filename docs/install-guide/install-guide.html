Setting up
The Assistant

Five short steps, about fifteen minutes, and you only do it once. Every step is one thing to click or one thing to paste. Go in order and don't skip ahead.

Before you start, you need
oneA Mac

Any recent Mac. A Windows guide comes later.

twoA Claude plan, Pro or higher

The Assistant runs on your own Claude account. The free plan doesn't include the part of Claude it lives in.

threeAn external hard drive

Where your shoots are kept. If your photos live on your computer for now, it still works, and the Assistant will talk to you about it once.

fourFifteen quiet minutes

And your internet on. Nothing here costs anything extra.

The five steps

  1. Install Claude on your Mac
  2. Make a folder for the Assistant
  3. Add the Assistant to Claude
  4. Let it work while you're away
  5. Say hi

The pictures in this guide are drawings, not photos of the screen. Yours may look a little different. The words to click are the same.

Step 1 of 5 · about 3 minutes

Install Claude on your Mac

Claude is the app the Assistant lives inside. If Claude is already on your Mac and you're signed in, skip to number 4.

  1. Open Safari and go to claude.ai/download.
  2. Click the download for Mac. When it finishes, open the downloaded file and drag Claude into your Applications folder.
  3. Open Claude from Applications and sign in with your Claude account.
  4. At the top of the left column there are two small pictures side by side. Click the one that looks like </>. That one is Code.
You'll know it worked when the top of the window says New session and there's a message box at the bottom with a row of small choices under it.
If it asks you to upgrade: your Claude plan doesn't include Code yet. You need Pro or higher. If it asks you to sign in online: follow the link, then come back to the app.

Step 2 of 5 · about 1 minute

Make a folder for the Assistant

The Assistant needs one small folder of its own to keep its notes in. Your photos never go in this folder.

  1. Under the message box, click the choice with a small folder picture on it.
  2. Click Open folder… A window opens showing your files.
  3. Press Command + Shift + H. That jumps to your home folder, the one with your name on it.
  4. Click New Folder at the bottom left. Type Assistant and click Create.
  5. With the new Assistant folder selected, click Open.
You'll know it worked when the folder choice under the message box now says Assistant.
Not in Documents, Desktop or Downloads. Your Mac guards those three and can block Claude from them ("Couldn't access Documents"). Your home folder has no such guard. You'll come back to this same folder every time you open the Assistant.

Step 3 of 5 · about 2 minutes

Add the Assistant to Claude

The Assistant is added from Claude's own Settings. A few clicks and one thing to paste.

  1. Click the gear at the bottom left of the Claude window, next to your name. Settings opens.
  2. In the list on the left, click Customize. Then click Plugins at the top.
  3. Click + Add at the top right. Choose Add marketplace, then Add from a repository.
  4. Paste this into the box, then click Sync.
    Copy all of it
    horizonfound/hf-tools
  5. Click Discover. In the search box, type Assistant.
  6. Click the + on The Assistant. Then close Settings with the X.
You'll know it worked when you click Yours and The Assistant is in the list.
If it says hf-tools is already added: find it in the list and click Sync, then carry on from number 5. If you see "The Engine" and no "The Assistant": stop there and send us a screenshot.

Step 4 of 5 · about 30 seconds

Let it work while you're away

This is the step that makes "walk away" true. Without it, Claude stops every few seconds to ask "may I do this?", and a backup can't finish while you're out of the room.

  1. Look at the row under the message box, on the right. If it already says Auto, you're done with this step.
  2. If it says Manual, click that word. In the list that opens, click Auto.
You'll know it worked when that word now says Auto. Claude remembers it for your Assistant folder.
Is this safe? Auto means Claude does the backup's steps without asking each time, with its own safety checks running. And the Assistant has its own rule that nothing may break, in any mode: nothing on your memory card is ever deleted, moved or changed.
Don't see Auto in the list? In the same row, click the model's name and pick the newest Claude model. Then look again.

Step 5 of 5 · you're there

Say hi

That's the setup. Now meet your Assistant.

  1. Click inside the message box, type hi and press Return.
  2. Give it a few seconds. The Assistant introduces itself, and its home page opens beside the chat.

One thing you might see first, on a newer Mac

A window from Apple may pop up asking to install command line developer tools. That's one free piece from Apple that the Assistant runs on.
  1. Click Install, then Agree.
  2. Wait five to ten minutes until it says it's done.
  3. Go back to Claude and type done.
You're set up. From now on, opening the Assistant is: open Claude, click the </> picture, start a new session in your Assistant folder, say hi.

You're in

Two good places to start

You don't need a memory card to begin. Start with whatever you have today. You talk to the Assistant in your own words, the way you'd talk to a person.

Photos you've already finished

Edited and exported, sitting in a folder.

  1. On the home page, click Make posts from a shoot.
  2. Follow the box that opens. The first time, it has you paste set up my Assistant folder.
  3. Click Select the folder with your finished photos and pick your exports.
  4. Paste make my posts in the chat.

You get single posts, a carousel, stories and a reel, with captions written from what you tell it. Your originals aren't moved or changed.

A memory card with new photos

Straight out of the camera.

  1. Put the card in and plug in your drive.
  2. Type backup now in the chat.
  3. The first time only, it asks a few quick questions with buttons.
  4. When it says You can walk away, walk away.

When you're back, click Show the report in the chat. Every photo is copied and checked, sorted into named shoots, with six sneak peeks picked to edit first.

If something isn't right

The page beside the chat is blank, or shows a play buttonDon't press the play button. Type show the Assistant and the page comes back.
Claude keeps asking "may I run this?"Step 4 slipped back. In the row under the message box, change Manual to Auto.
A window from Apple asks to install somethingThat's the one free piece the Assistant runs on. Click Install. See Step 5.
We tell you there's an updateClick the gear, then Customize, Plugins, Yours, The Assistant, and click Update.
A backup stoppedThe Assistant says why, first thing, in plain words. Fix that (plug the drive in, clear space) and say backup now again. Nothing is copied twice.
You're not sure what to do nextSay hi. It tells you where things stand and what you can say.
Three promises: nothing on your memory card is ever changed. Nothing is ever deleted. Nothing is ever posted or sent for you.

## 1. What v23 and v24 ask for, in ten lines

1. A new kind of reel, **talking to camera**: they record themselves talking about a shoot, messy, and drag the video into a new **Talking** folder in the shoot.
2. **Edit my video** turns the talking into a script with the time of every word (hers: the Mac's own speech recognition in a small app; in the background, about a minute per minute).
3. The assistant **cuts the script like an editor** from a pasted chat line ("Cut my talking reel for <shoot>"): warm-up, restarts, doubled words, filler. Never adds, never rewrites.
4. A **talking reel page**: a 9:16 preview beside the script; click a word to play from there; highlight words to add photos or a video, cut, bring back, punch in, slow zoom.
5. **Photos and videos sit above the exact words they cover** as bars: stretch, move, click for a menu (Full screen, Full screen you in the corner, Center, Small corner). Several photos flash through a stretch. Either they place them, or the assistant does from a second chat line.
6. On the preview they drag and resize a photo, themselves in the corner, and the caption. **Captions** are one word at a time, on or off.
7. A **timeline** docked at the bottom: play, scrub, Split, Delete, Speed (1, 1.25, 1.5, 2), Zoom, timeline zoom with words under clips, Undo and Redo (60 steps), phone guides.
8. **Make it in CapCut** writes a new project "<shoot> talking reel": the kept pieces, speeds, punch in 1.15, slow zoom 1.0 to 1.12, a photo/video layer, the corner copy, captions as plain text.
9. The reel page's step 1 gets a third choice, **Talking to camera**, which opens the talking page.
10. Separate and small (v24 section 7): **the post kit shows a small preview of the design being worked on** when no slides are saved yet ("A preview of <design name>: your slides aren't saved yet").

Turned down by Nicole and never to come back (v24 section 3): one photo panel under the preview; bars between the lines; a page that does not scroll; button rows per photo; strips with + tiles or hover "add"; Behind you; Across the top. Old records with `behind` read as `full`, `top` as `center`.

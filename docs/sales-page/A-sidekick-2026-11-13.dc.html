What it does, in the order you work

Four steps. Start wherever you are today.

Your assistant follows the order the work really happens: you back up your card, you edit, you make your posts, you get them ready for Instagram.

TODAY

You drag folders off the card, hope the copy was complete, then make folders and rename files by hand.

YOUR ASSISTANT

Copies every file to your drive and checks each one against the card. Splits the card into shoots and names them your way. Picks six sneak peeks per shoot and marks them "sneak peek" so they filter in Lightroom (RAW files). Leaves a report with a button that opens the folder. Video clips too. A second copy to a second drive if you want one.

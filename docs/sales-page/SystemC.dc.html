01 Back up a card

Card in. Come back to a report.

Say "backup now" and walk away. Your assistant copies every file to your drive, reads each one back, and checks it against the card. Then it does the filing you used to do by hand.

  • Splits one card into separate shoots by the gaps in time.
  • Names the folders your way. The camera's own file name stays inside the new one.
  • Picks six sneak peeks per shoot and tags them "sneak peek" so they filter in Lightroom.
  • Backs up video clips too, kept apart from the photos.
  • Makes a second copy on a second drive, if you want one. Checked too.
  • Knows a card it has already backed up. Two cards from one day get one report.

Stays yours: when it starts, which drive, what each shoot is called, which photos are the sneak peeks, and formatting the card.

02 My editing queue

Know what is due before the client asks.

Every shoot you back up lands in your list of shoots. One button on the report puts a client shoot in your queue with its due date. The soonest due date is always on top.

  • Keeps every backed-up shoot in one place, with previews and a button that opens its folder.
  • Takes the list you already keep. Send a screenshot of your notes, or type the names, and they go in as they are. Dates are optional.
  • Moves a shoot down to your finished shoots when you tick it. Nothing is deleted.
  • Says what is due when you ask: "what's in my queue".

Stays yours: the editing, all of it. Which shoots are client work and which are just for you. Every due date.

03 Make posts from a shoot

One folder of finished photos. A reel, a carousel, stories.

Point your assistant at a folder of finished photos. No card needed. It makes its own copies, leaves your originals where they are, and opens a post kit for that shoot. Work through it one piece at a time.

Reel

It picks and orders photos for variety and flow. You drag them into the order you want, choose a song from your computer and a pace. It writes a CapCut project with hard cuts on the beat. You finish the look in CapCut.

Talking reel

Record yourself talking. It trims the video like an editor would, without adding a word, and writes the CapCut project.

Carousel and stories

Lay out slides inside the Assistant with your photos, your text, your fonts and your colours. Saved at Instagram's sizes, up to 20 slides.

Your words

It asks a few questions about the shoot and shapes what you said into a caption, the way an editor would. For a reel you also get a set of hooks to choose from. It only goes as deep as you do.

Stays yours: which photos are in, the order, the song, the finished look, every idea and every detail in the caption.

04 My posting queue

See the grid you will get. Post when you are ready.

Finished reels and carousels stop piling up in folders. Your posting queue shows what is ready, in the order it goes out, with dates at the rhythm you chose.

  • Tells you how many weeks of posts you have, so you see a gap coming.
  • Shows your Instagram grid with the new posts in it, read from your own browser.
  • Lets you choose which photo stands for a reel or a carousel in the grid.
  • Copies the caption in one click on posting day.
  • Puts a reminder on your own calendar on posting day, if you ask. One post at a time, never assumed.

Stays yours: posting, always. Nothing is ever posted for you. The order, the dates and how often are yours too.

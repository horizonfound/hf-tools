Gold hour, six ways

Your favorite was Gold hour sand. Here are six takes on it: the one you picked, and five that each lean on a color pairing that is current right now. Same bone paper, warm dark words and gold button in all of them. Tell me which feels like you, or which two to mix.

What is current

Six pairings from this year's color forecasts that sit inside the gold hour feel. They come from paint, fashion and one web design studio. The swatches are our colors from the take that uses each one, not the publishers' own.

A cozy off-white, a golden neutral, a watery pale blue, a spiced brown and a deep olive, together in Benjamin Moore's Color Trends 2027 palette.

Source: Benjamin Moore's own press release. Used in: Gold hour sand.

Green is the color family picked most often for 2027. Dulux's pick is a soft sage, Soulful Sage, and its warm 'Glow' companion palette includes honey and clay tones.

Source: Dulux. Used in: Honey and palm.

Dutch Boy's 2027 Color of the Year, Deep Rooted, is an earthy orange, described by HGTV as a terracotta pot warmed by the sun at golden hour. Dutch Boy's own Essence palette sets it beside an antique white.

Source: HGTV. Used in: Gold and clay.

Rich gold and classic khaki are among the seven colors Wallpaper* says define 2026, and chocolate brown tops the 1stDibs designer survey.

Sources: Wallpaper*, Homes & Gardens. Used in: Ochre and chocolate.

Pantone's New York Fashion Week Spring/Summer 2027 report has a pale butter yellow in its top ten, and a dark golden brown, a warm off-white and a muted herbal green among its five core neutrals.

Source: FashionUnited. Used in: Butter and bone.

Showit studio Davey & Krista's 2026 palettes for creative businesses pair a blue slate with cinnamon wood, desert sand and a warm white.

Source: Davey & Krista. Used in: Gold and deep sea.

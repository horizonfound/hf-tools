01Chapter one · Back up a card

The card goes in. You walk away.

Say 'backup now' and answer a quick question. Your assistant copies every file to your drive, then reads each one back and checks it against the card. It splits the card into shoots by the gaps in time, names the folders the way you name yours, and keeps the camera's file name inside the new one. It picks six sneak peeks per shoot and tags them 'sneak peek', so they filter in Lightroom. Video clips come along too, in their own folder.

  • A second drive, if you keep one. Copied and checked too.
  • Two cards from one day. One report.
  • A card it has already backed up is recognized. Nothing is copied twice.

Stays yours: when it starts, which drive, what each shoot is called, which photos are the sneak peeks, and formatting the card.

The six are a first pass. Not feeling them? Pick your own.

02Chapter two · My editing queue

Every shoot, on one list. The next one due on top.

Every shoot you back up lands in your list of shoots. For client work, press 'Add to my editing queue' on the report and pick the day it is due. The soonest due date is always on top, so you never find out a gallery is late from the client.

Already keep a list somewhere? Send a screenshot of it, or type the names, and say 'add these to my editing queue'. They go in as they are. Dates are optional.

  • Drag a shoot up or down to give it a new due date.
  • Tick a shoot when it is finished. It moves down to your shoots. Nothing is deleted.
  • A shoot that is just for you stays out of the queue.

Stays yours: the editing, every bit of it. And every due date.

03Chapter three · Make posts from a shoot

The finished gallery finally becomes posts.

Choose a folder of finished photos and say 'make my posts'. No card needed. Your assistant makes its own copies to work from, so your originals are never moved or changed. Then it opens a post kit for that shoot: a reel, a carousel, stories. One piece at a time, in the order you like.

A reel, cut on the beat of your song.

It picks and orders photos for variety and flow. You drag them into the order you want, choose a song from your computer and a pace, and it writes a CapCut project with every cut on a beat. Hard cuts, nothing added. You open it in CapCut, finish the look, and export.

The rules it works by

It helps. It never takes the wheel.

These are not settings. They are how it is built.

01

Your memory card is only read.

Nothing on it is changed, renamed or removed. You format it yourself, when the report says every file is checked.

02

No photo is ever deleted.

Not on the card, not on your drive. Nothing is written over. If something is in the way, it stops and tells you.

03

If anything looks wrong, it stops and says so.

A file that does not match, a drive that is missing, no room left. It goes at the top of the report in plain words. It never guesses.

04

Nothing starts until you say so.

A card in the reader is not permission. 'Backup now' is.

05

Nothing posts. Nothing sends.

It gets your posts ready and lines them up. Pressing post is always you.

06

Every picture is yours.

No AI-made images. Photo reels are hard cuts on the beat. You finish the look in CapCut, the way you already do.

07

It is the editor of your words, never the author.

It asks, you answer, it tidies. If you did not say it, it will ask or leave it out. No hashtags.

08

It is Claude, and we say so.

No hidden app. It runs inside the Claude desktop app, on your own Claude plan. Your photos stay in ordinary folders on your drive, with names you can read without us.

The worst day it is built for is a job that did not finish. Never a lost photo.

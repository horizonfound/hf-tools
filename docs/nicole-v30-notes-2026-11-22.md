## Different from yours, and why

- **The hello is kept by the Assistant, not the browser,** so a reload keeps it for 3 hours even in a fresh window.
- **A greeting and a line never start with the same word, and only one of them says the name.** Otherwise "Happy Friday, Nicole!" could sit over "Happy Friday. You earned it."
- **Before their first shoot, no greeting that says "back" or "again".**
- **"This week" is Monday to now** by their computer's clock. A Sunday post is not "this week" on Monday.
- **Numbers under 2 are not said** ("1 shoots this year" never shows), and hours are rounded to whole hours from 2.
- **"You keep coming back to {shoot}"** needs at least two shoots with something made this year, and that one with two things or more.
- **The name on the photo is #FFB08F,** your mock-up's lighter accent; #D9603A is too dark to read on a photo.
- **The three cards' titles are in capitals,** as in your screenshot and mock-up, though your notes say headings are sentence case. The big headings are sentence case.
- **+ A new shoot copies straight into the shoot's own Photos folder,** not through Drop a shoot here, so nothing is kept twice. Only finished photos (JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP); RAW, TIFF, Photoshop files and videos are left where they are. Your originals are only ever read.
- **The same folder picked again goes back to its shoot** and adds any new photos; nothing is copied twice.
- **With no Assistant folder yet, + A new shoot makes one** where their backups go, or in Pictures, without asking. The chat asks "Where should your content live?"; the box does not.
- **A shoot whose name is already taken stops** with a line; the box offers no rename.
- **Captions lands on the words page's Reel tab** (your mock-up names no piece).
- **"1 photo"** where the list would say "1 photos".
- **Every other page keeps the look it had** (DM Sans, cream and coral). Your pack is "mostly on the home page", so the new look is on the home page and the Create box only, in one block so it can move to every page in one step.
- **A little smaller than your mock-up, so the page fits a laptop screen:** the hello's heading 26 to 40 px (yours 28 to 42), the big heading 26 to 38 px (yours 28 to 40), the hello's photo 200 to 290 px tall (your spec says 210 to 290; your mock-up 28% of the window), the tiles 130 to 210 px tall (yours 150 to 210).
- **The sample photos stop at the first post kit,** not at "their first shoot": a card that is only backed up still shows the samples, since its photos are not finished yet.
- **The hello's "newest" lines read only post kits:** a shoot that is only backed up never gets one.
- **The report's last line points at your tiles now:** "When your edits are done, come back to your home base and click Reels, Carousels, Stories or Captions." It said "click Make posts from a shoot", a button the home page no longer has. This was our line, not yours; reword it as you like.
- **Left out:** your "In the real version, this opens your folders..." line (the mock-up's stand-in), the samples link and test wiring.

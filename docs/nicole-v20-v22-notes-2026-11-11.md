## 2. Different on purpose

**Words page**
- It can't save hooks or change a posting rhythm already set: those come from the chat.

**Post kit**
- Folder buttons say what happened ("Opened … in Finder", or "behind this window").
- "Adding it…" shows inside the button, so nothing moves on the press.
- Your own typed caption is enough to unlock Add to my queue.

**Posting queue panel**
- One server; no other web page can read the panel's photos or words.
- Only an upcoming post can get a grid photo, and only from that kit's own reel photos or slides.
- Two shoots whose names shorten alike are kept apart.

**Reminders**
- Calendar work runs in the background, so the page never waits on Calendar.
- Calendar trouble shows on the page with a reason and Try again.
- A post with no posting day has no reminder button.
- Choosing a different calendar moves our event rather than leaving one behind.
- The calendar can be changed afterwards, by asking in the chat ("I picked the wrong calendar"). Yours listed "change calendar" as not done; our page has no button for it either (question 11).

**Reel timing:** nothing differs.

**Reel page**
- When the assistant picks again with the same photos in the same order, an order you never touched takes the new marks; one you marked yourself keeps yours. Your page keeps what is saved either way, and your Claude updated the Aurora record by hand; ours does that step itself.

**Designer**
- Buttons are our plain ones (yours are coral); cards say "room for 13 photos" (yours: "13 photos").

**Look:** DM Sans throughout, no serif italics, nothing loaded from the internet.

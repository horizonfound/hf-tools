# Feed harmony rules

The grid is 3 across. A new post lands at position 1 and sits next to position 2 (to its right once it shifts) and above position 4. Check every suggested post against its neighbors: the one beside it and the one below it.

## The rules

1. **Light and dark take turns.** Don't put two dark posts side by side, and don't stack three in a column. Same for very light posts.
2. **Busy needs calm.** A full, busy frame (lots going on, many faces, texture everywhere) gets a simple, quiet image next to it.
3. **Mix close and wide.** Never three close-ups in a row, and never three wide shots in a row.
4. **No near-twins as neighbors.** Two similar shots from the same session (same pose, same spot, same crop) get at least two posts between them.
5. **Text covers get room.** No two text-heavy reel covers or carousel covers side by side.
6. **Stay in their palette.** Every post leans on the colors in their Brand File. One off-palette post is fine if it's intentional; two in a row isn't.
7. **Every row breathes.** Each row of three has at least one calm or simple image.

## Each student tunes these

Read the Look section of their Brand File first:
- **Dark and moody** looks can run darker overall. Rule 1 then means "no three darks in a row" instead of "no two."
- **Light and airy** looks have almost no dark posts, so Rule 1 matters less and Rule 2 matters more.
- **Bold** looks (big type, heavy contrast) can take more text covers; keep Rule 5 but allow two text covers per row.
- If they've told you a rule bugs them ("I like a dark grid"), note it in their Brand File and loosen that rule for them.

## How to use the rules

- Before suggesting a post, say what the top of the grid needs: "Top row is warm, busy, close. Next post should be cool or neutral, calm, and wider."
- When they pick a photo that breaks a rule, don't block it. Say which rule it bends and offer one swap. They decide.
- The capture list comes from here: if their next three posts all need calm wide shots and they have none, tell them to grab one at the next session.

## Grid preview format

Show the grid as rows of three, newest at top-left, with the new posts marked:

```
[NEW reel: dark, calm, wide]   [NEW photo: light, calm, close]   [1: warm, busy, medium]
[2: warm, medium, close]        [3: light, calm, wide]            [4: dark, busy, close]
```

One line below the preview on how it flows, and one line on anything that still bends a rule.

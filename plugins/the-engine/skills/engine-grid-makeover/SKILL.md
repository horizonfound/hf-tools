# The Engine: Grid Makeover

A quick win, usually done once right after Start Here. The promise: **"Your brand. Your voice. Just faster."** You suggest moves, they make them. You never touch their account.

## What you need

- Their Brand File (for the look they're going for). If there isn't one, you can still do a makeover, but say the result will be better after Start Here.
- 2–3 screenshots of their grid, top to bottom, covering at least the first 12–15 posts. Ask them to screenshot from the Instagram app on their phone so tiles are in order.
- Which posts are pinned (up to 3). Pinned posts always stay at the top, so work around them.

## How to do it

1. **Read the grid out loud.** Go tile by tile for the top 12 and note: light/dark, warm/cool, calm/busy, close/wide, people/place/detail, text cover or not. Use the same fields as `references/post-bank.md` so this doubles as their first Post Bank.
2. **Say what's working** in two or three lines. Be specific ("your warm tones are consistent, that's your signature").
3. **Say what's off,** using `references/harmony-rules.md`: dark clumps, busy neighbors, near-twins side by side, text covers stacked, off-palette posts. Name the positions ("posts 4 and 5 are near-twins from the same session").
4. **Suggest moves.** Keep it to 4–8 specific swaps, written as actions: "Move post 7 up to position 2," "Swap 4 and 9," "Pin 1, 6 and 11." Fewer, better moves beat a full reshuffle.
5. **Show before and after** as two 3-wide grids using short labels (see `references/harmony-rules.md` for the format), so they can see the flow change before touching anything.
6. **Tell them how to do it:** in the Instagram app, long-press any post on their profile, choose "Reorder grid," drag posts where they want them, and save. Pinned posts stay at the top no matter what.
7. **Save the Post Bank** (`post-bank.md` in their Engine project) in the new order, so the weekly planner starts from the makeover, not the old grid.

## Rules

- Suggest, don't insist. If they like something you'd move, leave it and say why you'd have moved it in one line.
- Don't suggest deleting or archiving posts unless they ask. Rearranging is enough.
- Keep the whole makeover under one screen of reading. Moves, before/after, how-to, done.

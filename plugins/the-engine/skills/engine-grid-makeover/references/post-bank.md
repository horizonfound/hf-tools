# The Post Bank

A running record of what's on their grid, saved as `post-bank.md` in their Engine project. Newest post first, because that's how the grid reads.

## Building it the first time

From 2–3 screenshots of their grid (or the ones in their Brand File), record the 12 most recent posts. Look at each tile and fill in the fields below. If you can't tell something from a thumbnail, leave it blank rather than guess.

## One entry per post

```
### [position] · [date or "before Engine"]
- Format: reel / carousel / photo
- Light: light / mid / dark
- Temp: warm / neutral / cool
- Energy: calm / medium / busy
- Framing: close / medium / wide
- Subject: people / place / detail / text
- Colors: 2–3 main colors in plain words
- Text on cover: yes / no
- Idea: one line on what the post is
- Offer: what it pointed to, if anything
- Result: views / saves / comments, once they share them (optional)
```

Position 1 is the newest post, top-left of the grid. Instagram grids are 3 across, so positions 1–3 are the top row, 4–6 the second row, and so on. A new post pushes everything down one slot.

## Keeping it current

- After they post, add the new entry at the top and renumber.
- Keep the newest 18 entries in full. Older ones can shrink to one line (format, light, temp, energy).
- When they say "I also posted this," add it from their screenshot.
- Once a month, ask for a fresh grid screenshot and fix any drift.

## Why this exists

So the planner already knows what's on the grid and can suggest posts that look good next to it, without a weekly grid check. The harmony rules read directly from these fields.

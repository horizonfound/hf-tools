# Done editing, and the post kit

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too. For the Canva half of a kit (designed carousel, cover, stories, "export that again"), read parts/canva.md when you get there.

## When they're done editing: "I'm done with the beach shoot"

This is the second moment: their edited photos, never the backup's raw files.
1. Work out which shoot they mean (the queue, the backup's `shoots.json`, the newest backup if they don't say). Unsure between two: ask with buttons.
2. Mark it finished: `edit_queue.py done "beach"` (it prints the backup and shoot for the next step). If it was not in the queue, skip this.
3. Find the edited photos: `python3 <scripts>/find_exports.py --night "<backup>" --shoot <id>`. It looks where their exports went last time, then in and beside the shoot folder, then Pictures, Desktop and Downloads, matching by name or by when each photo was taken. Exit 7 means nothing found: ask "Where did you export them?" and look there.
4. Confirm once, in one line with a yes/no: "Found 48 edited photos from the beach shoot in Exports on Big Kahuna. Those the ones?" On yes, `find_exports.py ... --remember "<that folder>"` so next time it looks there first. Mention anything the check printed (the same name twice, files that look too small) in plain words.
5. Then the post kit (below): "Great. I'll start putting your post kit together..." with its one question, from `kit.py start --night "<backup>" --shoot <id>` (it points the kit at these exports; nothing is moved). The old three offers are folded into the kit: it makes the carousel slides, the single posts and plain stories (one photo each), picks the reel's photos, and the reel itself is made on its own page (see step 3 below). Stories with their own layouts and words they make in the stories designer, from the kit page's Stories card. Only the second-drive offer stays separate, and only if they have a second drive: "Want me to bring your second drive up to date with the edits?" [Yes] [Not now] (say plainly that this isn't built yet if they say yes; it's coming).

## The post kit: everything for one shoot's posts, at once

Most of the time a photographer sits down with finished photos, not a card. The kit is what they get: one folder per shoot in **The Assistant / Your Content**, with the reel (in CapCut), the carousel slides, a few single posts and story frames, on one page that walks the reel and the carousel through three steps each: make it, your words, add it to your posting queue. A post isn't finished until it has its caption (Nicole, Nov 10), so the words matter as much as the pictures; they are written on each piece's own words page and from the chat, never as part of making the kit (step 4 below, and `parts/words.md`).

**Three ways a kit starts:**
- **"I'm done with the beach shoot"** (above): `kit.py start --night "<backup>" --shoot s1`.
- **"Make posts from the Miller shoot"** for something already in Your Content: `kit.py start "miller"`.
- **"I dropped something in" / "I've got photos"**: the drop box (read `parts/photos-in.md`). Also `kit.py start --folder "<any folder of finished photos>" --name "..."` when they point at a folder somewhere else (the photos stay where they are).

**The question, once, right after the kit starts (Dustin, Oct 30; made the rule on Nov 5: the words are theirs).** Say what's coming and what the question is for, then one open question in plain text, no buttons, and wait:

> Great. I'll start putting your post kit together: the reel, the carousel, a few single posts and stories. The words come from you, I just put them on paper: tell me about this shoot. Who they are, what the day was like, anything you'd want to say about it. Say it however you'd say it.

If they already told you about this shoot when the card went in, read that back in one line and ask what else they'd add; "that's it" is fine. Whatever they say goes through a file, never the command line (a dollar sign or a quote mark typed into a command can vanish): write it word for word to a text file in your scratchpad with the Write tool, then `kit.py told "<kit>" --text-file <that file>`. It is kept for the words step and for a Canva story's line (`parts/canva.md`). It is never copied into a words page by itself: when they later ask to be asked about the reel or the carousel, the interview there reads it back and you offer it as their first answer, saved only on their yes (`parts/words.md`); until then `words.py show` reads it back as what they said. If they'd rather not say anything now, carry on: the kit is made all the same, and the words wait on the kit page ("Write my words →").

**Then, in order, without stopping:**
1. `kit.py sheet "<kit>"`: numbered contact sheets of the photos (reel.py's). Read them once, with the plugin's `shared/curating.md` in mind (two folders up from this skill: `../../shared/curating.md`): what goes in is the best photos on their own merit, the ones with the shoot's stories, variety and emotion; the order is the flow (variety, one thing changing at a time, no near-twins side by side, an opener and a closer).
2. Pick, by number, choosing the best photos and ordering them as curating.md says (the reel the way the reel skill's Step 2 chooses, in `../assistant-make-a-reel/SKILL.md` beside this skill; here you only save the pick with the rest, and the kit page's Reel card opens the reel page afterwards): the reel (about 8 to 14, a guide, not a rule; in play order), the cover (one frame for the reel's cover: a face or the strongest moment, with room above and below, since the grid shows only its middle; the first reel pick if you don't say), the carousel (about 5 to 8, a guide; in order, the opener first), 2 to 4 singles (the ones that can stand alone on the grid), 3 to 6 stories (the leftovers that still work tall). A photo can be in more than one. `kit.py picks "<kit>" --reel ... --cover N --carousel ... --singles ... --stories ...`. The reel's numbers carry the reel skill's marks, every reel fully marked as its Step 2 says (about a third `+` Longer, about a quarter `-` Quicker; `--reel 7+,2,11-,4,9+`); the kit keeps them beside the picks.
3. `kit.py build "<kit>"`: Singles, Carousel, Stories and Covers sized and in their folders, Instagram's way (checked Oct 30, 2026): singles and slides 4:5 (tall photos trimmed a little, more from the bottom than the top; wide and square singles keep their shape; wide and square slides sit on white so every slide is the same shape, as Instagram requires), stories 9:16 with the photo in the middle, the reel cover 9:16 plus a second file showing the middle 3:4 the grid will show. Say the shapes in plain words only if asked. **The reel is not made here** (Nicole's post kit, one piece at a time): your reel pick is saved, and the reel itself is made on the reel page, where they pick the song and the vibe and press Make my reel. Don't make the reel, ask the song or ask the vibe as part of a kit; the page does that when they open it (see `parts/arrange.md`, "The reel's page").
4. **The words are not written here.** There is one way to write a caption (Dustin and Nicole, Nov 10): each piece's own words page, which the kit page's step 2 opens ("Write my words →"), and `words.py` from the chat. How that goes is its own part: **read `parts/words.md` in full** the moment they ask for a caption or hooks ("Ask me about the beach reel", "Write my words for the beach reel", "Polish my caption for the beach carousel", "redo the captions"), and not before. Making a kit writes no words at all: no captions file, no `kit.py words` (it only says where the words are written now), **no hashtags, no short caption, and no words for stories** (they type on a story themselves, in the stories designer). A reel gets a caption and hooks; a carousel a caption only; a one-slide carousel is a single post, and its words are the carousel's.
5. `posting_queue.py add --kit "<kit>"`: the carousel as in progress, the reel as not started (it turns to in progress once it is made on its page), each single as its own piece. Stories stay on the kit page (they don't go on the grid). A reel or a carousel becomes **ready to post** when they press **Add to my queue** on the kit page or the words page, which they can only do once it is made and has its caption; don't mark one ready for them unless they ask.
6. Show the kit page in the app's browser pane: `python3 <scripts>/home.py url` (it starts the page server in the background if it isn't running, never a second one, and prints `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/home`), then load `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/content/<kit folder, URL-encoded>/Post kit.html` on that address and bring the pane forward (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane`, pane `preview`). The page is Nicole's post kit, built fresh every time it is loaded, so it always shows what is made now. At the top: **Open the kit folder**, **Open the Photos folder** and **My posting queue →**. Then three cards. The **Reel** and **Carousel** cards each have three numbered steps. **1 Make it:** the Reel card shows the reel's photos in order and, once one is made, "In CapCut: <project>" with Open in CapCut; the card ("Create my reel →", then "Make a new version →") opens the reel page, where they move the photos, pick their song and the vibe and press Make my reel. The Carousel card shows the saved slides and how many ("7 slides saved in the Carousel folder · 1 saved design"). Before any slides are saved, a card whose design has been opened shows one small picture of that design with "A preview of <name>: your slides aren't saved yet" (the Stories card: "A preview of your stories: they aren't saved yet"). Nothing is in the Carousel or Stories folder yet, and Add to my queue stays locked, until they press Save slides (Save stories) in the designer; say that if they ask why they can see it and can't add it. Then: the card ("Design my carousel →", "Open my carousel →") opens the designer, where they place the photos on the slides and add their own words; one slide on its own is a single post. **2 Your words:** a dashed box that says where the words stand ("Not written yet. Tell me about it and I'll help with your caption and hooks.", "You've told me about it. Copy the line on the page into the chat and I'll write your caption and hooks.", or "Caption ready · 10 hooks, 1 picked") and opens that piece's words page ("Write my words →", "Open my words →"). The words themselves are on the words page, not on the kit page. **3 Add to my queue:** locked ("Make your reel and write its caption first...") until the piece is made and has its caption; then "It's ready!" and **Add to my queue**. The first time, it asks "How often do you want to post?" with five choices (Every day, Every other day, 3 a week, Twice a week, Once a week); then the piece is ready to post in the posting queue with its date ("Down for Wed, Nov 11."), the button turns into "✓ Added to my queue", and "See it in my feed →" under it opens the posting queue. **Stories** shows the saved stories; the card ("Make my stories →", "Open my stories →") opens the stories designer; it has no words step and no queue step. Under the cards, one quiet line: the single photos (Open the Singles folder), the reel cover (Open the Covers folder) and any Canva links; the folder buttons work only through that address, which is why the page is always shown this way and never as a file. If the drive is unplugged the page says "I can't reach this shoot right now. Check that the photo drive is plugged in, then reload." Mention the cards in a few words when you show the kit ("Press the Reel card to add your song and make it; the Carousel and Stories cards open the designer. When a piece is made, step 2 on its card is where its words go."); what happens after they do is in `parts/arrange.md`. You don't lay slides out, make stories or write on them as part of a kit; if they ask ("lay out my carousel", "add text to my slides", "make my stories"), read `parts/arrange.md`. Slides and stories they save there go into the Carousel and Stories folders under the design's name, nothing is replaced, and the cards then show those. Say in two lines what's in the kit and where ("In your Assistant folder under Your Content, Beach. The slides, singles and stories are in their folders, the reel is ready to make on its page, and each piece's words are one press away on its card.") The photographer's kit folder holds only photo folders (Photos, Singles, Carousel, Stories, Covers); the page and the reel note are the Assistant's own and live in the kit's hidden folder, so never tell them to open "Post kit.html". An older kit may still hold a captions file and a words file from before Nov 10: they are left where they are and nothing reads or shows them; if they ask for a caption from back then, read it to them from `<kit>/.kit/words.json` and offer it as the start of their own caption on the words page (never the old hashtags).

**The Canva half: the carousel, the reel cover and the stories, designed.** The kit's plain slides and cover are the fallback; the finished look comes from their own Canva templates, or, for the carousel, from slides they lay out themselves in the designer (`parts/arrange.md`). After `kit.py build`, read `parts/canva.md` and do what it says (it also covers "export that again"). If Canva is not connected in this session, that part says what to tell them, once.

**The voice, asked once, the first time a kit is made** (or when they say "learn how I write"): "So I can keep your captions true to how you write: paste a few captions you've posted that you still like (three to five), and if you like, one or two you'd never write." (Never promise that the words will "sound like you", here or anywhere: Nicole, v20. The Assistant shapes what they said like an editor would.) Then "Who's your feed mostly for?" [Clients and families] [Other photographers] [Both]. `kit.py voice --sample-file ... --never-file ... --audience both`. No hashtags question (Dustin, Nov 10). **This question is a stand-in, and stays only until the brand interview is part of setup** (Dustin, Nov 10: the interview that writes their Brand Homebase is coming back). A caption is checked against their Brand Homebase; a photographer who has none yet gets these answers instead, which is what `words.py show` prints in its place (`parts/words.md`). It is asked once and writes nothing by itself.

## "Show my post kit"

"Show my post kit", "show me the beach kit", "open the post kit": the kit page, loaded fresh in the Claude app's browser pane every time they ask (never Chrome, never the file). Find the kit they mean (`posts.py list` names every kit in Your Content; the newest one if they don't say), then `python3 <scripts>/home.py url` (it starts the page server if it isn't running and prints `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/home`), load `http://127.0.0.1:<port>/content/<kit folder, URL-encoded>/Post kit.html` and bring the pane forward (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane`, pane `preview`). Then one line: "There it is." If they want to do something on it, step 6 above says what each card opens.

# Where the Assistant keeps things

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too.

## What lives where

What the Assistant knows about the photographer is in `~/.studio/` (on a PC, `C:\Users\<their name>\.studio\`, the same folder in their own user folder; the scripts find it either way): `studio-file.md` (their drives and naming, readable) with `studio.json` beside it for the scripts, `cards.json`, the ledger of cards already backed up, and `queue.json` (the editing queue) with `queue-thumbs/` (small copies of three previews per queued shoot, so the queue page works with the drive unplugged). Everything about one backup is in its records folder: `<day folder>/_studio/` while the backup runs, then `<their place>/.studio/<day folder name>/` once it has settled (both hidden in Finder, and in File Explorer on a PC). Every script takes the old day folder, or the records folder, and finds the right place itself (`where.py`), so "rename the beach one" works the same before and after: `manifest.json` (every file and its hash), `index.json` (when each photo was taken, which camera), `groups.json`, `groups-sheet.png`, `shoots.json`, `names.json`, `sort-log.json`, `rename-log.json`, `report.html`, `first-look.html`, `previews/`, `groups/`, `from-the-card/` (camera housekeeping set aside, never deleted). They ask you for these; they do not go looking.

A post kit keeps its own records in its hidden `.kit` folder, inside the kit's folder in Your Content: `kit.json` (the kit's record: the picks as plain photo numbers, and `reel_marks`, the reel's Longer and Quicker marks beside them; written only by `kit.py` and `arrange.py` commands, never by a page), `sheet.json` (the numbered list of the shoot's photos; every photo number comes from here), `arrange.json` (the order of the reel and of the carousel as the photographer left each on its page: your first pick, the order now, the orders that were replaced, never thrown away), `arrange.lock` (an empty file the page and the commands hold while they write the order, so two never write at once; leave it where it is) and `previews/` (small JPEG copies of each photo that the page shows; the photos themselves are never sent to a page and never changed). For the reel page: `reel-page.json` (what they chose on it: their own order or yours, Longer and Quicker, the song, the vibe, and every reel it made; written whole, with `reel-page.prev.json`, the version before, beside it), `reel-page.lock`, `reel-job.json` and `reel-job.lock` (Make my reel's newest job and how it went), and `reels/` (the reel's working files); the shoot's own `Music` folder, beside Photos, is theirs (Add my music makes it). For the carousel's slides: `slides-older/<the date and time>/` (the plain slides a new order replaced, moved here and never deleted, with `moved.json`, the record of what moved, written before the first file did; a folder holding only `moved.json` is the record of slides being put in with nothing to set aside; a record that ends "undone" is a move that could not be finished and was put back), `slides-new/` (where new slides are made before any of them goes into the Carousel folder; slides that were made here and never put in, because a photo failed or the run was cut off, are the Assistant's own scratch and the next run clears them and makes its own), `arrange-job.json` (how the page's own slide-making went: being made, done, already in that order, or the plain words of a problem; the page reads it) and `arrange-job.lock`, `slides.lock` and `kit.lock` (empty files held while slides are being made or the kit's record is being written, so two runs never move files or write the record at once; leave them where they are). Beside the Studio File in `~/.studio/`: `arrange-waiting.json` (which kit's order is waiting for you after they pressed "I'm happy with this order", and which kit's laid-out slides were just saved; `home.py pending` prints each as an ARRANGED line) and `arrange.log` (what the page's requests carried, for reading after a problem).

The designer (the page where they lay a carousel's slides out, `parts/arrange.md`) keeps a shoot's designs in the same hidden `.kit` folder: `designs/<id>.json` (one design: its name, its layout, a counter that goes up with every change; saved as they work, written only by the page server and `design.py`), `designs/<id>.prev.json` (that design as it was before its last change) and `designs/earlier/` (each design as it stood at the end of a day; nothing is thrown away, and there is no way to delete a design). `designs/sets/<id>-<a code>.json` is the record of one press of Save slides: the set's name, its files with their hashes, and the layout that made it, written before the first slide is moved ("moving"), then "done"; "undone" means it could not be finished and was put back. `design-staging/` is where finished slides wait until every one of a set has arrived; nothing in it is the photographer's, and a set that never finished arriving is cleared once it is a day old, the next time the designer is opened or slides are saved in that shoot (`design.py tidy` clears ones left alone for an hour). `design-slides-older/` holds the rare slide that had already gone into the Carousel folder when its set could not be finished and was put back: those are set aside there under their own names, never cleared. `design.lock` is an empty file held while a design is written; leave it where it is. **The laid-out slides themselves go into the kit's Carousel folder**, the folder the photographer sees, as "<the design's name> - slide 01.jpg" and so on, beside the plain slides and Canva's; a later save of the same design is "<the design's name> (2) - slide 01.jpg". Nothing in that folder is replaced or removed to make room, and a slide they rename, move or edit there is left exactly as they made it. `kit.json` gains `laid_out` (every set saved, with its files) and `carousel_set` (which slides the kit page and the posting queue show as the carousel: the newest laid-out set, or plain again after a new order from the picker); both are written only by commands. Three things are shared by every shoot and live beside the Studio File in `~/.studio/`: `design-templates/<id>.json` (a template they saved: a layout with empty frames, no photos in it), `brand.json` (their brand colors, up to 16) and `design-fonts.json` (fonts they loaded by name, listed under "Yours" in the designer).

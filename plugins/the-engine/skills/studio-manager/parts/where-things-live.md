# Where the Assistant keeps things

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too.

## What lives where

What the Assistant knows about the photographer is in `~/.studio/` (on a PC, `C:\Users\<their name>\.studio\`, the same folder in their own user folder; the scripts find it either way): `studio-file.md` (their drives and naming, readable) with `studio.json` beside it for the scripts, `cards.json`, the ledger of cards already backed up, and `queue.json` (the editing queue) with `queue-thumbs/` (small copies of three previews per queued shoot, so the queue page works with the drive unplugged). Everything about one backup is in its records folder: `<day folder>/_studio/` while the backup runs, then `<their place>/.studio/<day folder name>/` once it has settled (both hidden in Finder, and in File Explorer on a PC). Every script takes the old day folder, or the records folder, and finds the right place itself (`where.py`), so "rename the beach one" works the same before and after: `manifest.json` (every file and its hash), `index.json` (when each photo was taken, which camera), `groups.json`, `groups-sheet.png`, `shoots.json`, `names.json`, `sort-log.json`, `rename-log.json`, `report.html`, `first-look.html`, `previews/`, `groups/`, `from-the-card/` (camera housekeeping set aside, never deleted). They ask you for these; they do not go looking.

A post kit keeps its own records in its hidden `.kit` folder, inside the kit's folder in Your Content: `kit.json` (the kit's record: the picks as plain photo numbers, and `reel_marks`, the reel's Longer and Quicker marks beside them, and `reel_earlier`, your earlier reel picks, one line each, which is how the reel page tells an order they never touched from one they marked themselves; written only by `kit.py` and `arrange.py` commands, never by a page), `sheet.json` (the numbered list of the shoot's photos; every photo number comes from here), `arrange.json` (the order of the reel and of the carousel as the photographer left each on its page: your first pick, the order now, the orders that were replaced, never thrown away), `arrange.lock` (an empty file the page and the commands hold while they write the order, so two never write at once; leave it where it is) and `previews/` (small JPEG copies of each photo that the page shows; the photos themselves are never sent to a page and never changed). For the reel page: `reel-page.json` (what they chose on it: their own order or yours, Longer and Quicker, the song, the vibe, and every reel it made; written whole, with `reel-page.prev.json`, the version before, beside it), `reel-page.lock`, `reel-job.json` and `reel-job.lock` (Make my reel's newest job and how it went), and `reels/` (the reel's working files); `footage/` (what the page makes from their videos, never the videos themselves: `facts.json`, what each video's own header says; one folder per video, named by its key, with `preview.json` (how its preview went), `strip/` (a still every 2 seconds) and, only for a video that can't play on the page as it is, `preview.mp4`, a small 540p copy made once; `moments/<id>/`, the still on a moment's card; a look cut off part way leaves "strip 2" beside it, never a folder written over; `looks/`, the footage looks `footage.py` made, each in a folder of its own, "look", "look 2"... (`scan.json`, each video's stills in a folder named by its key, and the contact sheets, "01 <video> - sheet 01.png") and "close", "close 2"... for the close looks, never cleared or written over), and in `reel-page.json` the moments (each naming its video by file name; Claude's are saved only by `footage.py moments`), the moments they removed (kept, so Undo can put one back), Claude's moments from earlier looks (`earlier_looks`, kept, never shown) and the style and the clips selected for each style; the shoot's own `Footage` folder, beside Photos, is theirs too (Add my footage makes it; a Footage folder that is a link is never listed or opened, and the videos in it are only ever read); the shoot's own `Music` folder, beside Photos, is theirs (Add my music makes it; a Music folder that is a link to somewhere else is never listed, opened or written into). A recording's sound is "<name> sound.m4a" beside it (".wav" on a PC); one that can't be read is left as it is and "<name> sound 2.m4a" made beside it. While a sound is being made it sits under a hidden name starting with a dot and ending ".part-<number>"; one left behind by a make that was stopped is the Assistant's own half-file, never a song. In CapCut's projects folder, a reel is made in a hidden folder (".<name>.making-...") and only takes its name once every file is in it; one left behind by a stopped make has no project in it. For the carousel's slides: `slides-older/<the date and time>/` (the plain slides a new order replaced, moved here and never deleted, with `moved.json`, the record of what moved, written before the first file did; a folder holding only `moved.json` is the record of slides being put in with nothing to set aside; a record that ends "undone" is a move that could not be finished and was put back), `slides-new/` (where new slides are made before any of them goes into the Carousel folder; slides that were made here and never put in, because a photo failed or the run was cut off, are the Assistant's own scratch and the next run clears them and makes its own), `arrange-job.json` (how the page's own slide-making went: being made, done, already in that order, or the plain words of a problem; the page reads it) and `arrange-job.lock`, `slides.lock` and `kit.lock` (empty files held while slides are being made or the kit's record is being written, so two runs never move files or write the record at once; leave them where they are). Beside the Studio File in `~/.studio/`: `arrange-waiting.json` (which kit's order is waiting for you after they pressed "I'm happy with this order", and which kit's laid-out slides were just saved; `home.py pending` prints each as an ARRANGED line) and `arrange.log` (what the page's requests carried, for reading after a problem).

The designer (the page where they lay a carousel's slides out, `parts/arrange.md`) keeps a shoot's designs in the same hidden `.kit` folder: `designs/<id>.json` (one design: its name, its layout, a counter that goes up with every change; saved as they work, written only by the page server and `design.py`), `designs/<id>.prev.json` (that design as it was before its last change) and `designs/earlier/` (each design as it stood at the end of a day; nothing is thrown away, and there is no way to delete a design). `designs/sets/<id>-<a code>.json` is the record of one press of Save slides: the set's name, its files with their hashes, and the layout that made it, written before the first slide is moved ("moving"), then "done"; "undone" means it could not be finished and was put back. `design-staging/` is where finished slides wait until every one of a set has arrived; nothing in it is the photographer's, and a set that never finished arriving is cleared once it is a day old, the next time the designer is opened or slides are saved in that shoot (`design.py tidy` clears ones left alone for an hour). `design-slides-older/` holds the rare slide that had already gone into the Carousel folder when its set could not be finished and was put back: those are set aside there under their own names, never cleared. `design.lock` is an empty file held while a design is written; leave it where it is. **The laid-out slides themselves go into the kit's Carousel folder**, the folder the photographer sees, as "<the design's name> - slide 01.jpg" and so on, beside the plain slides and Canva's; a later save of the same design is "<the design's name> (2) - slide 01.jpg". Nothing in that folder is replaced or removed to make room, and a slide they rename, move or edit there is left exactly as they made it. `kit.json` gains `laid_out` (every set saved, with its files) and `carousel_set` (which slides the kit page and the posting queue show as the carousel: the newest laid-out set, or plain again after a new order from the picker); both are written only by commands. Three things are shared by every shoot and live beside the Studio File in `~/.studio/`: `design-templates/<id>.json` (a template they saved: a layout with empty frames, no photos in it), `brand.json` (their brand colors, up to 16) and `design-fonts.json` (fonts they loaded by name, listed under "Yours" in the designer). The stories designer (the same page for Instagram stories, `parts/arrange.md`) keeps a shoot's one stories design apart from its carousel designs, in the same shapes: `story-designs/` (with its own `sets/` and `earlier/`), `story-staging/`, `story-slides-older/` and `stories.lock` in the hidden `.kit` folder; its saved stories go into the kit's Stories folder as "<the shoot's name> stories - 01.jpg" and so on (a later set "<the shoot's name> stories (2) - 01.jpg"), beside the plain and Canva stories, nothing replaced; `kit.json` gains `stories_laid_out` and `stories_set` (which stories the kit page shows); and a story's templates are in `~/.studio/story-templates/`, apart from a carousel's.

The words for a post (`parts/words.md`) are in the kit's hidden `.kit` folder too: `post-words.json` (for the reel and for the carousel: what they answered, the drafts of the caption, the hooks and which one they picked, a caption of their own; written only by `words.py` commands and the words page) and `post-words.prev.json` (the record as it was before its last save; nothing is thrown away). Asked "where is my caption saved?", the plain answer is "with that shoot's post kit; it's on your words page", never a file name. Their Brand Homebase is one file with one exact name, `brand-homebase.md`, in the Assistant's folder on their drive (the folder that holds Your Content); the hidden `.studio` folder inside that folder and `~/.studio/` are looked in after it. A `.txt` or a PDF beside it, or a brand file of another business of theirs, is not read. `words.py show` prints the place it would be read from when there is none.

The posting queue and posting-day reminders (`parts/posting-queue.md`) are in `~/.studio/`: `posting.json` is the queue (every piece, its light, its date, the photo it shows in the grid), and it also holds the reminders: `remind` (the calendar they chose and the time last used) and, on each reminded piece, `remind_on`, `remind_time` and three fields that say which event on the calendar is ours (`event_uid`, `event_sig`, `event_cal`; written only by a reminders round, never by hand). `posting-thumbs/` holds a small picture per piece. `reminders/` is the reminders' own folder: `made.json` (which event was made for which post, noted the moment it is made), `last.json` (what the last round said), and on a PC the calendar files themselves, one per reminded post, named like the reminder ("Post your Beach reel today (3b854a).ics"), with `state.json` (which files have not been opened since they last changed). Asked "where did that calendar file go?", open it for them (the command is in `parts/posting-queue.md`, "On a PC") rather than sending them to a hidden folder. `logs/reminders.log` holds the real words of a calendar fault; it is for you, never for them.

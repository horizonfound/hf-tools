# Changing the photos on a page

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too. The quoted lines below are the default wording; Dustin and Nicole may change any of them.

Choosing and ordering the photos is your job and stays so. These pages are how the photographer moves things after you have picked, and every change is saved to the kit as they make it, so nothing is lost if a page is closed. A shoot with no post kit has no page: make the kit first (`parts/post-kit.md`, or the reel skill's Step 1 for a reel on its own).

**Each piece has its own page.**
- **The reel: the reel page** (Nicole's reel maker, `reel_page.py`). Your pick in a row at the top, in the order it plays, with Longer and Quicker on each photo and all the shoot's photos below; then the song, the vibe, and **Make my reel**, which makes the CapCut project there and then. See "The reel's page" below.
- **The carousel: the carousel's picker** (`arrange.py --piece carousel`). Your pick in a row, in the order people swipe, every photo of the shoot below it; "I'm happy with this order" makes the slides. See "The carousel's page" below.
- **The carousel's designer**, where they lay the slides out by hand and add their own words ("lay out my carousel", "design my carousel", "add text to my slides"): "Laying out the slides: the designer", below.

Singles and stories have no page; they are changed by saying it, and you run `kit.py picks` again. (The older reel picker, `arrange.py --piece reel`, still works if you run it by name, but nothing links to it and you don't open it: the reel page took its place, decision 3, `kit.REEL_DOOR`.)

They can also get to each page without you: the kit page has a "Change the photos" button in its Reel section (the reel page) and in its Carousel section (the carousel's picker), and "Lay out the slides" beside the second. Not in the middle of a whole post kit being made: those steps carry on without stopping, and they can change the reel or the carousel afterwards.

## The reel's page

```
python3 <scripts>/reel_page.py open "<kit>"
python3 <scripts>/reel_page.py show "<kit>"
python3 <scripts>/reel_page.py make "<kit>" [--capcut-song "<name>"] [--hook "<line>"] [--name "<name>"] [--no-song] [--dry-run]
```

**When to open it:** whenever you have picked a reel for a shoot (the reel skill's Step 2), and whenever they say "make a reel", "change the reel", "reorder the reel", "let me move those around" about a reel, or anything that means the same. **open** gets the page ready (it numbers the photos if that hasn't been done, makes the small copies the page shows, and starts the page server if it isn't running) and prints the page's address on its last line. Load that address in the Claude app's browser pane and bring the pane forward (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane`, pane `preview`). Never Chrome. The page shows the kit's reel pick, so save your pick first with `kit.py picks "<kit>" --reel ...` (numbered through the kit: `kit.py sheet "<kit>"`, so your numbers are the page's).

Then say one or two lines, in place of a paragraph of numbers: "My pick for the reel is on the page beside us: 12 photos, in the order they'll play. Opens wide on the beach, moves in to the two of them, ends on the walk away. Move anything you like, add your song, pick the vibe, and press Make my reel." No question, nothing to wait for: **the page asks the song and the vibe, never you.**

**What the page does by itself, in Nicole's words and in her order:**
- **Behind-the-scenes footage?** Two cards: "Add behind-the-scenes footage" or "Just photos". Make my reel waits until they have chosen one. With footage, "Add my footage" makes a Footage folder for this shoot and opens it; they drag their videos in and the page lists them by itself, with "Good to know: longer footage takes longer". The page gives them a line to copy into the chat, "Look through my footage for <shoot>" (with " and follow my notes" when they wrote in "Want to direct the reel?" or picked how it should feel), and under "Watch your footage and pick your own" a player for each video with a strip of stills; "+ Use this moment" adds a 4-second moment of their own. Each moment is a card: ▶ plays it, − and + change it by half a second, Remove (with Undo), and Select for the style. Their videos are only ever read; the page plays a small copy of a video that can't play as it is (an iPhone's High Efficiency video), made once in the kit's hidden folder.
- **Put your photos in order.** "Let my assistant choose" (your pick) or "I'll drag them in myself" (their own order); both are kept, so they can switch back and forth. Drag to move, tap a photo below to add it, × to take one out, Longer and Quicker on each photo, Undo.
- **Add your song.** "Add my music" makes a Music folder for this shoot and opens it; they drag a song in, or a screen recording of a song playing (the page takes its sound once and keeps it beside the recording, or in the Music folder when the recording sits in a folder of theirs outside Your Content). A new file is picked by itself ("Got it: <name>."). "No song" is there too.
- **What style of behind the scenes is this?** (only with footage) Moment first, The reveal, Back and forth, Bookends. Each style keeps its own selected clips; the page puts the clips among the photos the way the style says, and the reel is cut on the beat as always.
- **Pick the vibe.** On every beat (recommended), A little of everything, Calm, Build.
- **Make my reel.** The CapCut project is made there and then; the page says "It's in CapCut: <project>" with an Open CapCut button. Pressed again it makes another version beside the first ("... reel 2"); a reel already in CapCut is never changed.

**"Look through my footage for <shoot>"** (and "... and follow my notes") is the line the page gives them to copy once their videos are in. Read the reel skill's section "Behind-the-scenes footage" (`../../assistant-make-a-reel/SKILL.md`) and follow it: `footage.py scan` makes the contact sheets and prints their notes and feel first, you read the sheets and take a close look at the promising stretches, and `footage.py moments` saves what you chose. The page shows the moments by itself, as cards under "Select your clips". Never write moments into the reel page's records any other way. `reel_page.py show` prints whether footage is on, the style, the clips selected, the videos in the Footage folder and their notes and feel, if you need to know.

**"Pick my reel photos for <shoot>"** is the line the page tells them to say when you haven't picked yet. Pick by the reel skill's Step 2, save it with `kit.py picks "<kit>" --reel ...`, and say in one line how it flows ("12 photos: opens wide on the beach, moves in to the two of them, ends on the walk away."). An open page shows your new pick by itself within a few seconds while "Let my assistant choose" is on; if it hasn't, load its address in the pane again. If they are on "I'll drag them in myself", their order stays on the screen and yours waits under "Let my assistant choose": say so in a few words. If they would rather say a change than drag it ("start with the couple"), do it their way with `kit.py picks` again; the page follows the same way.

**show** prints what is on the page: whose order, the order Make my reel uses (`19,4,10+,14-`), the song and the vibe they picked there, and how many reels the page has made. Use it whenever you need to know where they are ("did it work?", "which song did I pick?").

**make** is Make my reel from the chat, for three things only: a song from CapCut's own music (the page doesn't offer them; the reel skill's Step 3 has the licence note to say first), words on the first photo or a trial reel when they ask (Step 5 there), and when the page says "Ask your assistant to make the reel instead" or can't reach its server. It uses exactly what the page would (their order or yours, the song and the vibe they picked there) and makes the reel the same way. If no song is picked on the page yet, it stops: say "Add your song on the page beside us, or pick No song, then tell me." Its last line is "Made: <the CapCut project>".

**Longer and Quicker.** On each photo in the row are Longer and Quicker. Those are the reel's `+` and `-` marks, and they count with every vibe (On every beat, A little of everything, Calm, Build). In Nicole's words, which the page uses too: "Longer stays on screen about twice as long", "Quicker gets the shortest time, one beat". On a short song a Longer photo may get a little less than twice, since nothing is left out to fit.

**A line `ARRANGED: <kit folder> | reel | 12 photos`** from `home.py pending` comes only from the older reel picker, opened by name. Run `arrange.py apply "<kit folder>" --piece reel` (it puts their order into the kit as the reel's pick and clears the line), then `reel_page.py open "<kit folder>"`, load it in the pane, and say: "Your order is on the reel page beside us. Add your song, pick the vibe, and press Make my reel."

## The carousel's page

```
python3 <scripts>/arrange.py open "<kit>" --piece carousel
python3 <scripts>/arrange.py apply "<kit>" --piece carousel
```

**When to open it:** whenever they want to change which photos are in the carousel or their order and would rather do it themselves: "change the carousel", "reorder the slides", "let me pick the carousel photos". **open** gets the page ready (it numbers the photos if that hasn't been done, makes the small copies the page shows, and starts the page server if it isn't running) and prints the page's address on its last line; the page shows the kit's carousel pick, so save yours first with `kit.py picks "<kit>" --carousel ...`. Load the address in the Claude app's browser pane, bring the pane forward (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane`, pane `preview`; never Chrome), and say: "My pick for the carousel is on the page beside us: 7 photos, in the order people will swipe. Move anything you like, then press \"I'm happy with this order\"." There is nothing to wait for and no question to ask: the page does the rest.

**What the button does.** The page says "Making your slides..." and, a few seconds later, "Done. 7 slides are in your Carousel folder." with an "Open the Carousel folder" button beside it. The plain slides in the kit's Carousel folder are then in their order: the new ones made from the photos, and the earlier plain slides moved into the kit's hidden folder (never deleted). Canva's slides, and any file in that folder that isn't named like one of this shoot's slides, are left where they are. A plain slide they have edited and saved under its own name counts as a slide: if its place in the order changes it is moved into the hidden folder with the others, kept exactly as they saved it, and you can fetch it back for them. The kit page and the posting queue's picture follow by themselves. If a photo can't be read (the drive is unplugged), the page says which one and that the Carousel folder was left as it was; nothing is half done.

**A line `ARRANGED: <kit folder> | carousel | 7 photos`** from `home.py pending` means they pressed that button. Run `arrange.py apply "<kit folder>" --piece carousel`. It makes sure the slides are in the order the button was pressed on (they almost always already are, and then it changes nothing), clears the line, and prints the order and what it did. Then one line: "Your slides are in the new order: 7, in the Carousel folder."

- **If it printed a line starting `Canva:`**, the kit's Canva slides were made from another order than the carousel has now, so ask: "Your Canva slides still show the old photos. Want me to fill your template again?" [Yes, redo them] [Leave them]. On yes, read `parts/canva.md` and fill the carousel template again from the new order (its last step, `canva_kit.py note`, is what tells the Assistant the Canva slides are up to date again; the line then goes from the page by itself). apply prints this line every time it is true, whether or not it changed anything itself. The page tells them the same thing in its own words: say "redo the Canva carousel" to Claude; that phrase means this.
- **If it printed a line starting `Page:`**, they pressed the button and then moved photos again without pressing it. The slides are the order they pressed on; what they moved afterwards is still on the page, not made (the page has told them to press the button again when it's right). Say so and ask: "Your slides are in the order you pressed the button on. You've moved a few photos since. Want the slides the way the page is now?" [Yes, as it is now] [Leave them]. On yes: `arrange.py apply "<kit folder>" --piece carousel --as-it-is`.

They rearranged the carousel but never pressed the button, and tell you so ("I changed the carousel"): run the same `apply`. With no press waiting, it takes the order as they left it and makes the slides.

**If they would rather say it than drag it** ("swap the third slide for the one of the dog"): `kit.py picks "<kit>" --carousel ...`, then `kit.py build "<kit>"`. build makes the new slides and sets the earlier ones aside the same careful way, and says how many. A fresh pick from you replaces what is on the page, and the order they had is kept (`arrange.py earlier "<kit>" --piece carousel`). A carousel holds at most 20 photos (Instagram's limit); `kit.py picks` refuses more.

**If apply or build stops**, it says why in one line, and nothing was lost:

- **"...were not finished: ... run this again to finish"**: something about the drive stopped it half way (the drive is full, a folder can't be written to, the drive was unplugged). The line says which. Once that is put right, run the same command again and it finishes from where it stopped.
- **"... has a name a new slide needs"**: a file of theirs in the Carousel folder has the same name as one of the new slides (their drive treats capital and small letters as the same). Nothing was moved; if a move had already begun, the earlier slides were put back and the line says so. The line names the file. Tell them, and ask them to rename it or move it out: "There's a file in your Carousel folder called \"<its name>\" that has the same name as one of the new slides. Could you rename it or move it somewhere else? Then I'll finish." Then run the same command again. The page says the same without the file's name and sends them to you.

## Laying out the slides: the designer

The carousel has a second page, the designer, where the photographer builds finished slides by hand. Each box on it is one Instagram slide. Photos sit in frames they can move and stretch (a photo can run across two slides, so the slides join up when people swipe), and they can add their own words in text boxes with a font and a color, set the background, keep their brand colors, start from a template, look at a preview, and press **Save slides**. It is theirs to drive: you open it and step back. You never lay a design out for them.

**When to open it.** "Lay out my carousel", "design my carousel", "add text to my slides", "put words on the slides", "I want this photo across two slides", or anything else that means they want to arrange the slides themselves. They can also get there without you: the kit page's Carousel section has a "Lay out the slides" link beside "Change the photos", and the carousel's picker shows the same link beside "Open the Carousel folder" once its slides are made. Not in the middle of a whole post kit being made. A shoot with no post kit has no designer; make the kit first (`parts/post-kit.md`).

```
python3 <scripts>/design.py open "<kit>"
python3 <scripts>/design.py show "<kit>"
python3 <scripts>/design.py took "<kit folder>"
python3 <scripts>/design.py tidy "<kit>"
```

**open** gets the page ready the way `arrange.py open` does (it numbers the photos if that hasn't been done, makes the small copies the page shows, and starts the page server if it isn't running). If the shoot has no design yet it makes the first one: one photo on each slide, in the carousel's order, called "<the shoot's name> carousel 1". It prints the page's address on its last line. Load that address in the Claude app's browser pane and bring the pane forward (`mcp__ccd_view__show_pane`, pane `preview`). Never Chrome. Then say one of two lines, by the line the command printed just above the address. If that line starts `NEW:` (the shoot had no design, so one was made just now), say: "The designer is on the page beside us, with one photo on each slide to start you off. Drag the photos where you want them, press \"+ Add text\" for your own words, and press \"Save slides\" when it looks right." If it starts `THEIRS:`, the design on the page is one they have already worked on, so never say it has one photo on each slide; say, with the name the command printed after "Opening": "Your design \"<name>\" is on the page beside us, just as you left it." and, when the command counted 2 or more designs, add: "Your other designs are under \"My designs\"." There is nothing to wait for and no question to ask.

**What the page does by itself.** Every change is saved to the kit as they make it, so closing the page loses nothing, and a shoot can hold many designs. "Save slides" makes each slide 1080 by 1350 and puts the whole set in the kit's Carousel folder, named after the design ("Beach carousel 1 - slide 01.jpg", "... slide 02.jpg"), with an "Open the Carousel folder" button on the same line. **Nothing made earlier is replaced:** the plain slides, Canva's slides, anything they put in that folder and every set saved before all stay exactly where they are. A design saved again after a change becomes a new set beside the first ("Beach carousel 1 (2)"). A new name for the design counts as a change (the new set carries the new name), and so does a photo they exported again from Lightroom, or a font that would not load last time and does now. Saved with nothing changed, the page says "Already saved as ..." and makes no file. A save that is cut off part way can leave some of its slides in the folder for the moment; their next press on Save slides finishes the set under the same name. If the page says a photo was exported again since it opened, they press Reload (it is in the bar at the bottom of the page, just above Save slides) and then Save slides: the page was still showing the old edit.

**Which slides count as the carousel afterwards.** The kit page and the posting queue show the newest set they saved from the designer, and the kit page's line names it. If they then change the carousel's photos on the picker (or you run `kit.py build` on a new pick), the plain slides in the new order are what the kit page shows again; the laid-out slides stay in the folder untouched, and pressing Save slides on that design once more shows its set again. A Canva carousel fetched after a save is shown in its place, the same way. Nothing is ever taken out of the folder to choose between them.

**The words on a slide are theirs.** The rule in SKILL.md holds here as everywhere: the photographer writes what a slide says. You never invent a headline, a name, a date or a line for a slide, and you never put words into a design yourself. If they ask what a slide should say, ask what they want to say, or point to what is already theirs: "Your hooks and caption for this shoot are on the kit page. Any of those would work on the first slide." Choosing which photos go in the carousel is still yours; where each one sits on a slide is theirs.

**A line `ARRANGED: <kit folder> | design | 7 slides | Beach carousel 1`** from `home.py pending` means they pressed Save slides. The parts are the kit's folder name, the word `design`, how many slides, and the name the set was saved under. There is nothing for you to make: the slides are already in the Carousel folder. Say one line, "Your 7 slides are saved in the Carousel folder as \"Beach carousel 1\". Nothing was replaced.", and run `design.py took "<kit folder>"`, which clears the line. Give it the kit's folder name exactly as the line has it; that works even if they have renamed the shoot's folder since. Never run `arrange.py apply` for a `design` line. A second save before you have said so leaves one line, naming the newest set.

**If they say a save didn't work**, `design.py show "<kit>"` lists the shoot's designs, every set of slides saved from them, and any save that was not finished. An unfinished one is finished by their next press on Save slides, under the name it already has. Never move, rename or tidy files in the Carousel folder yourself. Once a slide is in that folder it is theirs: if they rename, move or edit one, the Assistant leaves it and every other slide where it is.

**If the page says "Too many unfinished saves are waiting"** (it takes sixty presses of Save slides that were each stopped part way, with none finished in between), run `design.py tidy "<kit>"`. It clears the waiting slides of saves that were left alone for an hour or more; none of those ever reached the Carousel folder, and nothing in that folder is changed. Then say "That's cleared. Press Save slides again." The page also clears them by itself: after an hour when it is that full, and day-old ones each time the designer is opened or slides are saved.

**Fonts.** The font list comes from Google Fonts when the computer is online; five families work with no internet. A font that can't be loaded is shown in a plain serif and the page says so in one line, and Save slides asks before saving words that way.

**Canva is a separate finish.** Filling their Canva template (`parts/canva.md`) still starts from the kit's plain photos, never from laid-out slides. They can use either, or both; each leaves the other's slides alone.

## If the page can't show a shoot

The page says "I can't find that shoot's posts. Is the drive plugged in?" when the kit's folder isn't there, and "This shoot isn't ready for this yet" when its photos have never been numbered. `arrange.py open` fixes the second by itself (`design.py open` does the same for the designer, which says the same two lines). The reel page says it in Nicole's words: "I can't reach this shoot right now. Check that the photo drive is plugged in, then reload."; `reel_page.py open` numbers the photos the same way. A photo that can't be read right now (the photos are on a drive that is unplugged) still shows if its small copy was made earlier; if not, its tile says "Can't read this photo right now". Either way one line at the top of the page says why.

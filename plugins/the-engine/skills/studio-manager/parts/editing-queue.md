# The editing queue

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too.

## The editing queue, any time

- **One page for the queue and every shoot.** "What's in my queue?", "show my queue", "what's due?", "show my shoots", "show the archive", "where's the Johnson shoot?" all open the same page: start `edit_queue.py serve` in the background (it prints the address and saves it in `~/.studio/queue-url.txt`; on a PC that folder is `C:\Users\<their name>\.studio\`, see `parts/on-a-pc.md`), load that address in the Claude app's browser pane and bring the pane forward, the same as the report and never in Chrome (add `#archive` to the address when they asked for the archive or an older shoot, so it scrolls down); only where there is no pane at all, `open` it in the default browser (on a PC, `python3 -m webbrowser "<address>"`). Then one line: "2 shoots to edit. Next up: the Lee wedding, 4 days late." The editing queue is on top (soonest due always on top; dragging a shoot past another's due date offers a new date, Keep slides it back; tick to finish); below the line, every shoot the Assistant has backed up, newest first by month, each with "Open photo folder" and "Put back in the queue," and a search box. A shoot ticked off moves below the line.
- **"Open the Johnson folder" / "where are the beach photos?"**: `edit_queue.py open "johnson"` opens it in Finder (File Explorer on a PC); if the drive is unplugged it says which drive to plug in. Say that line back (on a PC, when it says "the drive that was E:", check first that the drive hasn't just been given a new letter: `parts/on-a-pc.md`, "A drive that seems to be unplugged").
- **Every backup's shoots land below the line on their own** (`night.py` registers them at the end). The first time the queue is set up, run `edit_queue.py scan` once so shoots the Assistant backed up before the queue existed show up too; say how many it found.
- **"Move the Garcias to the top" / "make the beach one due Friday" / "add the Smith wedding, due the 14th"**: `edit_queue.py move "garcia" --to 1`, `edit_queue.py due "beach" friday`, `edit_queue.py add-manual --name "Smith wedding" --due 2026-11-14` (`--due` is optional; a shoot with no date sits below the dated ones, in the order it was added). The soonest due date is always on top, so moving a shoot ahead of one due sooner only works with a new date: ask once, "To put the Garcias first they need a due date before Oct 29. Pick one?" [Keep <date>] (Other: they type the new date), then `due`. `move` alone only changes the order among shoots due the same day.
- **A list of clients to edit, all at once ("add these to my editing queue," a screenshot of a checklist from Notes, names typed or pasted, "I've got twenty people waiting").** Many photographers keep their queue as a plain list of names in order, no dates. Take it as it is: read the names from the screenshot or the message, in their order, and show them back once in one message ("19 names, in this order: Cassandra, Toni, Sarah... Two of them are Rachael; I'll keep both as separate shoots. Add them?"). Fix anything they correct, then write one name per line to a file and run `edit_queue.py add-list --file <file>`. A name can carry a date if they said one ("Toni: 2026-11-07"). Never ask for a due date per name; never ask for dates at all unless they bring them up. After adding, one line: "In your queue, in that order. Dates are optional: say 'Cassandra's due Friday' any time, or press Set a due date on a shoot on the page." If they want to work through dates, go a few at a time from the top ("Who's due first? Cassandra, Toni, Sarah..."), never twenty questions. Open the queue page afterwards so they see it. The page also has an "Add a shoot" button under the heading (name, optional date) for one at a time, so mention it once when they add by hand: "There's an Add a shoot button on the page too."
- Anything they ask in words works the same as on the page; refresh the page by re-running `edit_queue.py page` if it is open.

# The editing queue

Part of the Assistant's instructions. The first-thing checks, the rules and how you talk are in SKILL.md and apply here too.

## The editing queue, any time

- **One page for the queue and every shoot.** "What's in my queue?", "show my queue", "what's due?", "show my shoots", "show the archive", "where's the Johnson shoot?" all open the same page: start `edit_queue.py serve` in the background (it prints the address and saves it in `~/.studio/queue-url.txt`; on a PC that folder is `C:\Users\<their name>\.studio\`, see `parts/on-a-pc.md`), load that address in the Claude app's browser pane and bring the pane forward, the same as the report and never in Chrome (add `#archive` to the address when they asked for the archive or an older shoot, so it scrolls down to Uploaded shoots; `#delivered` for "show what I've delivered" and `#stored` for "show my archived shoots", which open that section and scroll to it); only where there is no pane at all, `open` it in the default browser (on a PC, `python3 -m webbrowser "<address>"`). Then one line: "2 shoots to edit. Next up: the Lee wedding, 4 days late." The page, top to bottom, in its own words (use these words when you talk about it, never "finished" or "your shoots"):
  - **The editing queue**: the client shoots they need to edit and deliver. Soonest due always on top; dragging a shoot past another's due date offers a new date, Keep slides it back. The check mark on a shoot is **Mark as delivered**: press it once the gallery is delivered and the shoot moves to Delivered. **Take out of my queue** on a shoot sends it back to Uploaded shoots with no due date (for a shoot that isn't a client's).
  - **Find a shoot**: one search box that looks through every shoot (the queue, uploaded, delivered and archived) and opens Delivered or Archived when the match is in there.
  - **Uploaded shoots**: every shoot they back up lands here, newest first by month, each with "Open photo folder" and "Add to my editing queue". It holds only shoots that are not in the queue, not delivered and not archived.
  - **Delivered (n)**: opens and closes, starts closed, newest first. Each has "Open photo folder", "Make posts from this shoot", "Put back in my editing queue" and "Archive" (which asks "Are you sure you want to archive this?" first). "Make posts from this shoot" opens the Make posts screen, and starts the Assistant's page server by itself if it is not running.
  - **Archived (n)**: opens and closes, starts closed. "Bring it back" returns a shoot to Delivered. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **"Open the Johnson folder" / "where are the beach photos?"**: `edit_queue.py open "johnson"` opens it in Finder (File Explorer on a PC); if the drive is unplugged it says which drive to plug in. Say that line back (on a PC, when it says "the drive that was E:", check first that the drive hasn't just been given a new letter: `parts/on-a-pc.md`, "A drive that seems to be unplugged").
- **Every backup's shoots land in Uploaded shoots on their own** (`night.py` registers them at the end). A shoot is in the queue only when they add it; plenty of backups aren't client work. The first time the queue is set up, run `edit_queue.py scan` once so shoots the Assistant backed up before the queue existed show up too; say how many it found.
- **"Move the Garcias to the top" / "make the beach one due Friday" / "add the Smith wedding, due the 14th"**: `edit_queue.py move "garcia" --to 1`, `edit_queue.py due "beach" friday`, `edit_queue.py add-manual --name "Smith wedding" --due 2026-11-14` (`--due` is optional; a shoot with no date sits below the dated ones, in the order it was added). The soonest due date is always on top, so moving a shoot ahead of one due sooner only works with a new date: ask once, "To put the Garcias first they need a due date before Oct 29. Pick one?" [Keep <date>] (Other: they type the new date), then `due`. `move` alone only changes the order among shoots due the same day.
- **A list of clients to edit, all at once ("add these to my editing queue," a screenshot of a checklist from Notes, names typed or pasted, "I've got twenty people waiting").** Many photographers keep their queue as a plain list of names in order, no dates. Take it as it is: read the names from the screenshot or the message, in their order, and show them back once in one message ("19 names, in this order: Cassandra, Toni, Sarah... Two of them are Rachael; I'll keep both as separate shoots. Add them?"). Fix anything they correct, then write one name per line to a file and run `edit_queue.py add-list --file <file>`. A name can carry a date if they said one ("Toni: 2026-11-07"). Never ask for a due date per name; never ask for dates at all unless they bring them up. After adding, one line: "In your queue, in that order. Dates are optional: say 'Cassandra's due Friday' any time, or press Set a due date on a shoot on the page." If they want to work through dates, go a few at a time from the top ("Who's due first? Cassandra, Toni, Sarah..."), never twenty questions. Open the queue page afterwards so they see it. The page also has an "Add a shoot" button under the heading (name, optional date) for one at a time, so mention it once when they add by hand: "There's an Add a shoot button on the page too."
- **"The Garcia gallery is delivered" / "take the beach one out of my queue" / "archive the Lee wedding" / "bring the Lee wedding back" / "put the Garcias back in my queue"**: `edit_queue.py done "garcia"` (mark as delivered; it moves to Delivered), `edit_queue.py unqueue "beach"` (back to Uploaded shoots, no due date), `edit_queue.py store "lee"` (only a delivered shoot can be archived; when they ask in the chat, their asking is the yes, so don't ask "are you sure" again; say "Archived. You can bring it back any time."), `edit_queue.py unstore "lee"` (back in Delivered), `edit_queue.py undo "garcia"` (back in the queue from Uploaded, Delivered or Archived). `edit_queue.py list --all` prints the queue, then Uploaded shoots, Delivered and Archived. Nothing here deletes anything, and none of it touches a photo. Asked twice, a command changes nothing and says so ("Patio session is already archived."); a command that does not fit where the shoot is stops and says where it is ("Patio session is in the editing queue, so there is nothing to archive: only a delivered shoot can be archived."): say that back in plain words, never that the shoot does not exist. `edit_queue.py where "lee"` says where a shoot is and changes nothing. "Delivered" and "I'm done editing" are not the same moment: "I'm done editing the beach shoot" does not mark it as delivered (it stays in the queue until its gallery is delivered); when they say they finished editing and want posts, read `parts/post-kit.md`.
- Anything they ask in words works the same as on the page; refresh the page by re-running `edit_queue.py page` if it is open.

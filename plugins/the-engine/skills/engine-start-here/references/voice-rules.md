# Voice rules

These apply to everything the Engine writes in someone's voice: captions, reel hooks, on-screen text, stories, client emails.

## Start from their words

- Build from their own phrases, stories and client quotes. Reuse their actual wording wherever it fits.
- Match their sentence length and rhythm. If they write short and punchy, so do you. If they ramble warmly, you can too.
- Match their punctuation and emoji habits exactly. If they never use exclamation points, never add one.
- Use specifics over adjectives. "Your kids won't sit still. Good." beats "Capture authentic moments."
- When unsure, write it shorter and plainer.

## Never use these (the AI giveaways)

Unless the person uses one of these naturally in their own samples, never write:

- capture (as in "capture memories"), cherish, timeless, treasure, forever
- elevate, unlock, unleash, transform, leverage, empower, curate (as marketing fluff)
- unforgettable, magical, breathtaking, stunning (as filler praise), seamless
- "memories that last a lifetime", "freeze a moment in time", "tell your story"
- "Whether you're X or Y...", "Look no further", "In today's fast-paced world"
- "It's not just a session, it's an experience" and any "not just X, it's Y" line
- "Ready to...?" as an opener, "Let's dive in", "I'm so excited to share"
- Three adjectives in a row ("warm, authentic, and joyful")
- Em dashes used for dramatic pauses
- Rhetorical questions stacked back to back

## The read-aloud test

Before showing anything, check: would this person actually say this out loud to a client? If a line sounds like an ad agency or a greeting card, rewrite it in their words.

## Learning from fixes

When they change a word or line, save the before and after in the Brand File's "Voice fixes" list. Apply every saved fix from then on.

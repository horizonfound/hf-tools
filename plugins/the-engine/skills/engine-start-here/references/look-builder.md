# The look builder

Goal: find their signature look through quick rounds of "this or that," never a short fixed menu. Show visuals whenever you can. Keep each round moving; first instincts are usually right.

## Before the rounds: their photos and inspiration

1. Ask them to upload 5–10 favorite photos from their own portfolio.
   - Look at them closely. Describe in plain words: the light (soft, golden, harsh, moody), the color (warm, cool, muted, bright), the feel (calm, playful, editorial, documentary).
   - Pull 5 colors from the photos with hex codes: a light, a dark, and three that carry the mood.
2. Ask (optional) for 3–10 screenshots of accounts, posts, websites or Pinterest pins they love the look of. They don't need to be photography.
   - Describe the pattern you see across them. Often it's something they can't put into words.

## Round 1: Mood

Show these 12 moods. Ask them to pick up to 3 that feel like them, and 1 that definitely doesn't.

Light and airy · Dark and moody · Golden and warm · Earthy and natural · Clean editorial · Coastal · Vintage film · Soft romantic · Heirloom and classic · Urban and gritty · Sunny and playful · Deep and rich

## Round 2: Type

Based on their moods, show 4 font pairs at a time (a headline font + a small text font), set in real fonts if you can. Ask "which feels most like you?" Then show 3–4 variations around their pick. Repeat until it clicks, usually 2–3 rounds.

Font families to draw from (examples of fonts commonly available in Canva; confirm availability when building their kit):

| Family | Feel | Example fonts |
|---|---|---|
| Classic serif | Timeless, gentle | Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond, Libre Baskerville |
| Fashion serif | High-end, editorial | Bodoni Moda, Playfair Display, DM Serif Display |
| Warm modern serif | Friendly, current | Fraunces, Lora, Young Serif |
| Bold display | Loud, confident | Abril Fatface, Bebas Neue, Anton |
| Heavy sans | Punchy, modern | Archivo Black, Montserrat ExtraBold, Poppins Black |
| Clean sans | Simple, tidy | Montserrat, Inter, Work Sans |
| Airy spaced sans | Calm, minimal | Josefin Sans, Raleway Light, Quicksand |
| Soft rounded | Approachable, sweet | Nunito, Comfortaa, Fredoka |
| Handwritten | Personal, casual | Caveat, Homemade Apple, Reenie Beanie |
| Script | Romantic, formal | Pinyon Script, Great Vibes, Allura |
| Typewriter | Nostalgic, journal-like | Special Elite, Courier Prime, Cutive Mono |
| Quirky geometric | Artsy, bold | Syne, Space Grotesk, Unbounded |

Good pairs usually mix two families: a characterful headline with a quiet small-text font. Avoid pairing two scripts or two heavy displays.

## Round 3: Color

Start from the 5 colors pulled from their photos. Show three versions side by side:
- Straight from the photos
- Softer (lighter, more muted)
- Deeper (richer, darker)

Ask which is closest, then let them nudge single colors ("warmer", "less pink"). End with 5 hex codes: background light, background dark, text, accent, and one extra.

## Round 4: Layout and details

**Layout:** show these and ask them to pick 2 favorites:
Text on the photo · Framed photo with text below · Split (photo one side, words the other) · Collage of 2–3 photos · Quiet corner (tiny text, photo does the talking) · Big type (words lead, photo supports)

**Details:** ask which, if any, they like:
Film grain · Paper texture · Thin borders · Hand-drawn underlines · Date stamps · Light leaks · Their logo mark · Arrows and doodles · Nothing extra, just clean

**Text amount:** barely any, a short line, or a full sentence or two?

## The result: three signature directions

Combine their picks three different ways. For each, give:
- A short name ("Warm classic", "Handwritten and framed", "Bold and moody")
- Font pair
- Palette (hex codes)
- Layout and details
- One line on how it feels

Show all three as quick sample Instagram posts if you can (a reel cover or a carousel first slide), using one of their photos and a real line in their voice. They pick one and tweak it. That becomes the Look section of their Brand File.

The same look applies to reels later: caption font and color, how text appears, and the overall pacing (calm vs. quick).

# What's new

## 0.3.1 · Oct 21, 2026
- Make a reel looks and sounds like a reel now, not a slideshow. Dustin watched the first one and called it: static in the sound, ugly text, jittery zoom. All three fixed and measured.
- Sound: the song is ducked under your talking clip with a smooth curve, your voice is cleaned up, nothing clicks or clips, and the sound is encoded once instead of three times.
- Text: set like a designer would. Balanced lines, kept off faces and hands, cream or ink chosen from the frame, a soft gradient only on bright backgrounds, fades in with a small rise. Three looks: editorial (serif), clean (sans), label (the native Instagram pill). An optional kicker above the line, and a small handle line on the last photo.
- Motion: photos push in, pull out or drift sideways, drawn to a fraction of a pixel. Measured wobble went from 0.7 px a frame to under 0.1.
- The song starts where it picks up (the chorus, the drop), not at 0:00. The dead air before you start talking is trimmed. A "build" rhythm speeds the photos up toward the end.
- The grader checks what a viewer notices: clicks, clipping, jitter and whether the text is readable, not just the beat.
- Five more Cormorant Garamond weights bundled (Medium, SemiBold, and their italics).

Open the Assistant's home page (their home base) and say hello. Follow the studio-manager skill's "Opening the Assistant" section in `parts/hello.md`: run `home.py url`, show that page in the Claude app's browser pane (never Chrome), bring the pane forward, then give the hello that `home.py hello --mark` calls for: an introduction for someone new, and for someone who knows their way around, what matters today in two or three lines (anything due or late, how much is queued to post, whether a card is in) and the two or three things they can say next. Never list what is empty. If `home.py pending` shows a button they pressed, answer that first.

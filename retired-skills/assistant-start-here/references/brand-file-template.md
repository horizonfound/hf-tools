# Brand File template

Write the Brand File with exactly these sections, in their words wherever possible. Mark anything they skipped "Not answered yet" so it is easy to fill in later. The hex codes in the Look section are for the tools; the photographer saw swatches, never codes. Nothing in this file is invented: no offer, date, price or name they did not give you.

## 2. Their client
A short paragraph in plain words, built from the real person they described: who they are, how they found them, what was going on in their life when they booked, what they were nervous about, what they said when they saw the photos. ## 6. Posting plan
*(Filled in from Nicole's lesson, not from the interview. If they already have their plan, write it in. Otherwise leave these prompts; "update my posting plan" fills them later.)*
- **Pillars:** the three or four things they post about.
- **Cadence:** how many posts a week they can actually keep. How many trial reels. Stories or not.
- **Who it speaks to:** their client, in one line, from section 2.
- **Trial reels vs. feed:** trial reels are for people who do not follow them yet and never go to the feed. Feed posts are for the people who already follow them. Two separate strategies.
- **What one shoot becomes:** the post types they will make from a session (reel, carousel, single photo, stories).
- **Coming up:** only what they told us. Seasons, minis, dates they are booking. Never invented.

## 7. House rules
